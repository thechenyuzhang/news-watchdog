## What to read / what to watch

**This week's theme:** what changes when one person can point dozens of agents at a problem, and who has to check the results.

1. **Pietro Miotti (Google SWE), "My AI Agents Found an Unmapped Maya Site"** (HN **443**, Nov 11) — build log. **New creator.**
   - Started from a travel daydream: about 1,200 km² of public NASA lidar strips turned into images, and **48 agents went through them one by one following a written guide** (no custom model or classifier). Overnight they flagged villages; a literature/archive check left one hilltop civic center with a plaza, an 8–12 m pyramid, a courtyard and a possible reservoir.
   - Agents also checked the raw point cloud, wrote the report and translated it into Spanish. INAH replied within 72 hours that no site is registered there. It's still unconfirmed until fieldwork. His point: "being a naive outsider might be a superpower now."
   ([pietromiotti.com](https://pietromiotti.com/latest/maya-site/) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50452455))

2. **Patrick Garrity (VulnCheck), "After 7 months, Glasswing overwhelms maintainers with underwhelming findings"** (Nov 9; HN **434**) — data-driven critique. **New creator.**
   - 442 CVEs attributable to Anthropic (~0.5% of 2026 CVEs), only 2 exploited in the wild. The ledger shows 37,966 findings, 217 fixed, 1,654+ duplicates and 419 withdrawn, yet the claimed true-positive rate rose to 94.2%.
   - curl's batch: 19 of 34 published reports are "not a vulnerability" and 6 are low severity. The real bottleneck in AI vuln-finding is triage, and the OSS Scanner moves it onto maintainers. Read it next to a16z's "The Exploit Was the Bottleneck" from last week.
   ([VulnCheck](https://www.vulncheck.com/blog/anthropic-glasswing-november-update) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50447074))

3. **Alex Wang, "What objective is reasoning solving?"** (HN 149) — technical essay. **New creator.**
   - Treats reasoning as pathfinding through token space: binary-reward RL is rejection-sampling fine-tuning scaled by the success rate. It raises the chance that *some* path lands, not that any single chain converges.
   - Practical upshot: forcing longer chains helps only up to a point, chains of thought aren't required to be faithful, and scaling RL "fuzzes" an imperfect verifier into reward hacking. A good mental model for anyone building verifiers for agents.
   ([alexwang.ai](https://alexwang.ai/posts/reasoning-as-pathfinding/))

4. **Google, Gemini 4 Carbon developer guide** — primary doc. Covers the model ID, thinking levels, `/goal` and `/swarm` in Antigravity, the `gemini-api-dev` migration skill, and the Nov 30 price change. Skim it before you test. ([AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/learn/gemini-4-carbon-developer-guide))

5. **Evan X. Merz, "AI usage generates a fragile codebase"** (HN, big comment thread) — opinion from a former Meta engineer: shallow AI-written tests, unneeded complexity and rushed reviews pile up as debt. Worth it for the counterexamples in the comments. ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50438318))
