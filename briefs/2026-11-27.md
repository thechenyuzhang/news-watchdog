## TLDR

1. **AI agents moved from forecast to incident report. Switzerland confirmed five agent-driven cyberattacks, including two successful intrusions at critical-infrastructure operators. Separately, Reuters reports hackers inside the US Air Force's Cloud One platform (Confirmed / Likely).**
   - **Switzerland (NCSC/BACS advisory, Tue Nov 24, plus director Florian Schütz on SRF, Thu).**
     - Five confirmed cases, four of them successful compromises. Three targeted critical-infrastructure operators (electricity, water and telecom per SRF), and two of those succeeded. No major outages were reported, and the attackers are unknown.
     - The attackers used "Agentic Offensive Frameworks". The NCSC says publicly reachable agents such as **ARTEX** and **CyberStrikeAI** have "almost doubled since October."
     - Its advice: patch internet-facing systems **within 24 hours**, require MFA or passkeys everywhere, and shrink what's exposed.
   - **US Air Force Cloud One (Reuters via CNBC, Fri).**
     - The intrusion was found "within the past month" during an upgrade. The attackers exploited a software vulnerability to reach the **control plane**.
     - Migrations are paused, updates to running apps are blocked and some admin rights were revoked. Cyber Command has been called in. Who did it, what they took and whether AI was involved are all unknown.
     - "Cloud One" and "Cyber Command" are trending on X in the US.
   - **Founder angle.** Yesterday's story was ARTEX in nation-state toolkits. Today a government confirms it works against real utilities. The NCSC's 24-hour patch window is the new bar for anything you expose to the internet. If you sell security, "agent-speed detection and response" now has a public case file.
   ([NCSC advisory](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/en/26-ki-agenten-1-en) · [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-ai/switzerland-reports-first-ai-based-cyberattacks-on-critical-infrastructure/92440400) · [CNA/Reuters](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/ai-agents-used-in-five-cyber-attacks-in-switzerland-government-says-6585191) · [CNBC: Cloud One](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/hackers-breach-us-air-force-flagship-cloud-one-computing-platform.html) · [X trends](https://x.com/search?q=Cloud%20One))

3. **The crypto thread got its first concrete paper: a *deterministic* subexponential algorithm for discrete logs on prime-order elliptic curves and in F_p\*. It is conditional on OpenAI's unrefereed October construction, and it does not break deployed ECC (Confirmed paper; claim unverified).**
   - **Paper.** Liu, Zhang and Ren (Zhejiang University State Key Lab of Blockchain and Data Security), IACR ePrint **2026/3020**, dated Nov 23.
     - Worst-case time 2^O(b/√log log b) = p^o(1), with zero random bits and no GRH. Its "sole unrefereed premise" is OpenAI's Cauchy–Hermite Boolean tensor contraction (October 2026).
     - The lab's Chinese release frames it as breaking a "55-year" p^1/2 barrier.
   - **Reality check.** The earlier randomized results it builds on (Akshima et al. and Kaarelson) say explicitly they give **no improved attacks on P-256, P-521 or Ed25519**; commenters estimate the speedup only kicks in around 2^(2^17). HN (138 points) reads it as complexity theory, not a practical break.
   - **Founder angle.** This is not the "bombshell" Aaronson's sources hinted at, but it's the paper trail moving toward deployed primitives. The crypto-agility one-pager from yesterday still stands.
   ([ePrint 2026/3020](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/3020) · [ZJU release (中文)](https://bcds.zju.edu.cn/2026/1123/c84892a3230318/page.htm) · [cryptology.city: ABH+26](https://cryptology.city/References/ABH+26---A-Subexponential-Time-Algorithm-for-Elliptic-Curve-Discrete-Logarithm) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50665968))

4. **Where do your "zero-retention" prompts actually go? An OpenRouter-funded researcher traced DeepSeek traffic from "US" providers Novita, StreamLake (Kuaishou) and Ant Digital to ByteDance's Ark (Likely; one researcher, with an attested test harness).**

     - Image downloads came from Volcano Engine (AS137718) and BytePlus Singapore IPs.

   - **Founder angle.** "Hosted in the US, ZDR" on a marketplace isn't provenance. For sensitive workloads, pin providers, ask for written sub-processor lists, and canary-test your route.

## What to read / what to watch

**Today's theme:** how institutions and craftspeople adapt when agents do the work: a company retrofitting its SDLC, a founder rereading Tocqueville, a hobbyist rediscovering joy, and a builder on two futures for software.

1. **Patrick Collison, "Democracy in America"** (Substack essay, Nov 25). **New creator to the K section** (Layer 2; Mullenweg and others amplified it).
   - **The argument.** Tocqueville credits America's success to its *moeurs*: townships, juries and associations where people practice judgment. If AI resolves our coordination problems, "we might get better outcomes, but will we get better people?" He warns of a benevolent "soft despotism" that keeps people in perpetual childhood.
   - **Why now:** it lands the same week as the Axios "handover" fight and Tracing Woodgrains' essay. A sitting founder-CEO is arguing for preserving human practice, not just outcomes.
   ([Collison](https://patrickcollison.substack.com/p/democracy-in-america) · [Mullenweg](https://ma.tt/2026/11/collison-on-ai/))

2. **Mark Khuzam (Netflix Commerce), "Retrofitting the SDLC for AI Coding Agents"** (QCon SF talk, InfoQ write-up, Nov 23). **New creator.** Found through Layer 2 ("lessons from coding agents production").
   - **What they did.** Their first agent test migration took four attempts. The team then wrote **45 ADRs "for people and for agents"**: context, decision, pattern, anti-pattern and AI-usage notes. They treat AGENTS.md like robots.txt to index 300+ docs.
   - **Review.** A pre-review agent's first version left 23 comments on one PR, starting with "Overall this PR looks great!" It now posts only verified blockers with evidence. CI itself stays deterministic.
   - **Founder use:** a concrete playbook for teams whose agents flail on internal conventions.
   ([InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/11/netflix-sdlc-ai-coding-agents/))
