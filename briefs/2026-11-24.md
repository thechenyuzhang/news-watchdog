2. **An internal Claude model produced a Lean proof that VP ≠ VNP over every field, the algebraic version of P vs NP. No human has checked it (Confirmed claim; correctness unverified).**
   - **What was posted.** At 1:43 PM PT Monday, Anthropic merged a 70-page paper and a 73,324-line Lean 4 development into `anthropics/formal-math`.
     - Main theorem: every arithmetic circuit for the permanent (over every field of characteristic other than 2), and for the Hamiltonian-cycle polynomial (over every field), needs superpolynomially many gates (an explicit bound with a (log n)^(1/3) term in the exponent). That separates VP from VNP. Valiant posed the conjecture in 1979.
     - The paper is explicit that this **does not** settle Boolean P vs NP.
     - Anthropic's own wording: "The proofs in this paper were found and written by AI. No human mathematician has checked the proofs."
   - **Independent machine checks (overnight).** A clean rebuild on Windows passed comparator with the second kernel nanoda and a full `leanchecker --fresh` replay (Horace-Maxwell repo). Carlos Toledo's live audit page calls the *formal claim* "certified" by machine.
   - **What's still open.** Both auditors stress the same gap: no expert has confirmed that `Statement.lean` (~100 lines) means what the paper claims.
   - **Context.** This is the third headline AI proof in five days, after RH and Navier–Stokes. Google DeepMind also posted Lean-verified quantum-algorithm speedups Monday (News/products).
   ([GitHub PR #51](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/pull/51) · [paper](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00954v1) · [independent check](https://github.com/Horace-Maxwell/vp-vnp-lean-independent-verification) · [Toledo audit](https://carlostoledo.co/vp-vnp/))

## What to read / what to watch

**Today's theme:** measurement and authority. Who gets to say a model is smarter, a proof is right, or an agent is allowed to act?

1. **micjm, "I, too, can make a chart of perplexity drops"** (Nov 22, 3-min read, HN 84). **Repeat creator; promoted to the standing radar today** (see note).
   - **The question.** OpenAI's chart in "Perplexity and Intelligence" measures perplexity on a fixed corpus of code, so "perplexity against what?"
   - **The test.** The author (helped by Astra 6.2, they note) found a paper that trained a 160M model on five data mixes that differ only in their share of code. The 75%-code model has much lower perplexity on code and much *higher* perplexity on non-code text.
   - **The conclusion is measured.** The chart isn't meaningless, but it needs "a lot more qualifications". OpenAI's own footnote admits code prediction "is a poor proxy" for GPT-2 and GPT-3.
   - **Pair it with** Suleyman's caveat that cross-lab numbers will need bits-per-byte and shared datasets.
   ([micjm](https://micjm.com/blog/lowering-perplexity-without-scaling/) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50607512))

2. **a16z (Justine Moore, Raghu Raghuram, Matt Bornstein), "Investing in Embo"**, plus Embo's own launch post (Nov 23, ~6-min read each). Fresh a16z piece.
   - **The thesis.** Robot demos "hide how fragile those skills still are". World models trained on internet video give robots general physical knowledge. Practicing inside the model ("imagined experience", as in Dreamer 4's offline Minecraft diamonds) turns that knowledge into recoverable skills.
   - **The stack.** One unified model pretrained from scratch at internet scale, fine-tuned on their own humanoid fleet's experience, post-trained with RL, both in imagination and in the real world.
   - **Read it for:** how a16z frames the VLA vs. LLM-as-controller vs. world-model split, which is useful if you pitch anything physical-AI.
   ([a16z](https://a16z.com/announcement/investing-in-embo/) · [Embo](https://embo.com/introducing-embo))

3. **Michael Harley, "LLM agents work for their parent companies, not for you"** (HN 369, 146 comments; dated Nov 1, trending now). **New creator.** Found by a Layer 2 query on agent trust.
   - **The story.** He built a Claude Code skill that finds an Instagram reel's artist on Bandcamp so he can buy the music. When Bandcamp added a challenge, the agent refused to work around it, but it would still help download the same song from YouTube.
   - **The argument.** The agent you pay for enforces its vendor's risk policy, not your intent. "My robot, which I pay a pretty penny for" isn't your fiduciary.
   - **Founder use:** a crisp user-side framing of why agent-policy transparency and portability (and Claude Approvals-style policy files) will matter to buyers.
   ([Michael Harley](https://michaelharley.net/posts/2026/11/01/llm-agents-work-for-their-parent-companies-not-for-you/) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50627340))
